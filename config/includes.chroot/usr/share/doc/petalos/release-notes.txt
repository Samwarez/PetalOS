PetalOS RC 1
===========

This release candidate includes the native Wayland desktop, selected encrypted
SYSTEM preferences, optional independent Private storage, RAM content folders,
VPN post-connect checks, and the corrected private playback audio clock.
Wrong Private passphrases reopen a fresh prompt with a warning graphic.

Known limits:
- Kad may report firewalled despite working VPN port-forwarding probes. This is
  accepted as a low-priority known issue; the experimental aMule patch is excluded.
- Slight choppiness with 1080p playback in QEMU needs a hardware follow-up.
- Configured Usenet transfers have not yet been tested.

The PetalOS Guide describes the storage and password layout. Functional tests
do not establish anonymity or the absence of forensic traces.
